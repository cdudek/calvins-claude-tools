While a session has a process, the Session page shall draw no Clear and no "Copy resume command". It shall draw one line instead: "Clear is for ended sessions. Close its terminal first." Background sessions read "Clear is for ended sessions. This one is still running. Resume comes back when it ends." Screen/state: Session, Live. Tag: `@F9`.
