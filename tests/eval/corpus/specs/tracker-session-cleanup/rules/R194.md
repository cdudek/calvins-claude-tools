The script's `list` shall collect ended sessions and linked worktrees that are each idle 30 days or more. A session is ended when its row in `claude agents --json` has a job `id` and no `pid`. A session's idle time runs from its last activity: the newest time its row and transcript give. A worktree's idle time is the newest of three times. They are its branch's last commit, its folder's own modified time and the last activity of every session that used it. An unknown time is not collected. Screen/state: skill, List. Tag: `@F48`.
