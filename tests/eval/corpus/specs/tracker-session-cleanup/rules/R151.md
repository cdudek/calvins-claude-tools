The mod shall take a session's last activity as the newest of three times. They are its last prompt, its last turn end and the last line of its transcript. If none is known, the Session page shall read "last activity unknown". The idle time is then unknown (F48). Screen/state: Session, Facts line 1. Tag: `@F71`.
