When the re-read lists the session with an `id` and no process, the mod shall run `claude rm <id>`. It shall show "✓ Left Claude Code's list. The conversation is kept." A row with no `id` (an interactive row) gets no `claude rm`: the mod treats it as not listed (F26). Screen/state: Session, Cleared. Tag: `@F25`.
