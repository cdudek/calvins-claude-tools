While another session holds a fresh lease, a mod shall not run `claude agents --json`. It shall read `agents.json` every 5 seconds. An `agents.json` older than 15 seconds, or one that cannot be read, counts as a failed read for F52 and F123. Screen/state: store; Band Stale; Home Stale. Tag: `@F129`.
