## Method and limits

- **Read:** all seven spec files, `intent.md`, `docs/ux/setup.md`, `docs/ux/owner-words.md`, `decisions.json`, `handover.md`, and milestone 1's `validation.md` and `review/r2-laws.md` (format only). Worked independently; no other reviewer's notes were opened.
- **Evidence rule:** a check whose evidence codes are all S, I, T or M is `n/a: no build yet` unless the spec states the value or the behaviour that decides it. Checks with C or N ran on the spec text.
- **What "pass" means here:** every check that could run on the spec text passed. The S, I, T and M checks that could not run are named in the Evidence column. A pass is not a pixel check.
- **Not run for any law:** contrast (L1.4, L12.2), alignment and gaps (L1.2, L13.1-L13.3), target size in CSS px (L7.1-L7.3), all timings (L6.1-L6.5, L7.7 timing half), cold walk (L20.1), accessibility-tree exposure of status text (L6.7: a terminal has no ARIA, so there is nothing to check in the spec).
- **Already resolved in `conformance.md`:** C1 to C15 are not re-raised unless the resolution left a law check failing. C2 (confirm) is still open and is re-raised as R2-03, because its recommended answer contradicts `intent.md`. C5 (Tidy leaves the pane) is raised once, as a missing sentence (R2-06), not as a pattern departure.
- **Severity:** Nielsen 0-4 from the checklist (4 Critical, 3 High, 2 Medium, 1 Low, 0 design choice). B2B escalation does not apply: there is no money, consent, access or mail action. Rung R3 of the precedence ladder (safety of the user's work) is what lifts R2-01 to R2-03. Contradictions between spec files that change what the build must do are rated by milestone 1's rule (3) only where they change a key route (R2-04); the others are rated by their effect.
- **Result:** no finding at severity 4. Four at 3 (R2-01 to R2-04), eleven at 2 (R2-05 to R2-14 and R2-16), fourteen at 1 (R2-15, R2-17 to R2-29), two design choices at 0 (R2-30, R2-31). 19 of the 30 law rows fail, 9 pass, 2 are n/a; 6 of the 14 B rows fail.
